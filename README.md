# Opal App
Vision OS app by OpalAI 

Opal AI is a spatial design visualization tool for set and film production. People can use this to easily view, navigate and share fly-throughs of their sets. Current spatial rendering & visualization tools are complicated to use due to poor UI/UX and disjointed design workflows:

Workflow:
- Step 1: Open 3D model in Unreal Engine or Blender**
    - model syncs in real-time with the spatial app (Vision Pro)
    - spatial designer adds a light object in the Unreal App, and the light is updated synchronously in the spatial app
- Step 2: Add Waypoints**
    - **A**dd in waypoints to the 3D model to enable flythroughs
- Step 3: Flythough**
    - This starts a subsequence of actions where you click on specific points of the model and can export a fly through video of a virtual camera going through the model
- Step 4: Export flythrough**
    - Film Director and Producer will use this to better understand the set
